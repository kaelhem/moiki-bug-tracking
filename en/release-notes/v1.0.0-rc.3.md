# v1.0.0-rc.2 - v1.0.0-rc.3

A few bug fixes reported through Sentry, plus a quick note about platform funding.

---

## Editor

### [feats]

* **Donation banner** on the studio page: Moiki runs thanks to your support. The banner disappears for users who have already contributed, and can be hidden at any time.
* **Donation modal redesigned**: clearer emphasis on the “starting at €1/month” pricing.

### [fix]

* Fixed a bug in the **interactive tutorials**: clicking “Continue” could accidentally close the media picker and break the tutorial flow.
* Fixed a crash in the **text editor** that could sometimes occur on Android. This should prevent random errors while typing with the virtual keyboard.

---

## Reader

### [fix]

* The **fullscreen** button is now hidden when the browser does not support it (which could happen on Safari...).
* Fixed an **iOS crash** related to the audio player in the story reader on some devices.

---

## Social Club

### [fix]

* Fixed a display issue where the **“Episode”** badge could sometimes remain visible on other cards when switching tabs.
